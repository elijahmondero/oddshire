# Oddshire

**Oddshire** is an autonomous, no-camera **9:16 TikTok Live 2.5D RPG & Gacha world** inspired by *Animal Crossing* and tabletop RPGs.

Hundreds of viewer avatars inhabit one seamless curved-horizon diorama simultaneously—voting in macro chapters, typing free-text RP actions resolved in 8-second AI batches, pulling Gacha capsules at the Plaza Shrine (`!pull`), battling for the Village Crown (`!throne`), and vying for the **Sky Master Throne** via cumulative gift bidding wars.

## Quick Preview (Interactive 9:16 Prototype)

Open [prototype.html](prototype.html) directly in any modern web browser to test:
- The **72px-cleared Top 65% Safe Broadcast HUD** with toggleable TikTok top & bottom overlays
- Simultaneous **8s RP batching** + **24s macro votes**
- **Windowed Cumulative Sky Master Auction**, Contested Overtime & Defend mechanics
- **Plaza Gacha Capsule Shrine (`!pull`)**, Blacksmith Gear Upgrades (`!upgrade`), and Contested Throne Duels (`!throne`)
- Multi-agent crowd & camera priority tests (3.0s cut cooldown + safe-zone frustum Y-clamp)
